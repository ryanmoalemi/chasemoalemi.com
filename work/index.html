Work

Most days I'm working with my brother Ryan on our Shopify stores, mostly auto parts. I'm the operations guy. I make sure orders go out, listings are right, and the repetitive stuff runs on its own.

The part I actually enjoy is the data. Auto parts sound simple until you see how many ways one listing can be wrong.

Lately I've been using AI to double check our catalog. It's a second set of eyes, not the final word.

When a part comes back, I want to know why. Usually it leads me to a listing I can fix.
