Work

I work with my brother, Ryan, on our Shopify stores, mostly auto parts. I run operations. Orders go out, listings stay accurate, and the repetitive work runs on its own.

What I like is the data. An auto parts listing looks simple until you see how many ways it can be wrong.

I use AI to double-check the catalog. Second set of eyes. Not the final word.

When a part comes back, I look for why. It usually leads to a listing I can fix.
