Hi, I'm Chase Moalemi.| |

I spend my time on cards, the Pokémon TCG, and whatever sport the calendar is currently serious about. The rest of the day I work with my brother Ryan, running an online auto parts shop and automating the tedious parts of it.

Free hours go to the cards and the games. Work hours go to orders, listings, and the jobs that should not need a person every time.

What I'm into Right now

What I'm into

Three interests. I have put real time into each, and I can be particular about all of them.

  • Card collecting

    Pokémon cards and sports cards. I like a collection with a point of view: what you chase, what you trade, and what you refuse to let go of once you have held it.

  • The Pokémon TCG

    I play the game, not just the market around it. Deck building, sequencing, and the minute a tidy plan meets a better one across the table.

  • Sports

    All of them, in their season. I follow the calendar as it overlaps, which means there is usually a game worth knowing about and a table I am taking too personally.

Right now

What actually has the hours, in the shop and out of it.

With my brother

An automotive parts shop on Shopify

My older brother Ryan and I run Shopify stores together. One of them sells automotive parts. My work is the operation under the storefront: orders, listings, and the repetitive back-office tasks that quietly wreck a week if you do them by hand. I automate those tasks. The aim is plain. A listing should stay accurate, an order should move without a scavenger hunt, and the two of us should spend our time on the exceptions. It is a small shop. That is not an excuse for a sloppy one.

  • The collection

    Building it with a filter. Chasing pulls, making trades, and getting pickier about which cards deserve a sleeve.

  • At the table

    More Pokémon TCG, played on purpose. I want to know why a line worked, especially on the nights it did not.

  • The sports year

    Following seasons as they hand off. I try to show up for the stretch that decides something, not only the highlight.

  • This site

    Designing chasemoalemi.com and writing it myself. If you searched my name, I would rather you landed here than on a blank page.
