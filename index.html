Current focus

Applying AI to auto parts inventory

Auto parts are a hard catalog to manage. One part can fit dozens of vehicles, every listing carries a long trail of fitment details, and a small mistake turns into a return. Ryan and I run our stores on Shopify, and my focus is the operation underneath them: inventory, listings, and order flow. Right now that means putting AI to work on the tedious parts, like cleaning up product data, keeping listings consistent, and catching problems before a customer does. The goal isn't automation for its own sake. It's a store that stays accurate as it grows, with people spending their time on the decisions that actually need them.
